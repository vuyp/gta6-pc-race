How the lanes and the order are read

A project that is in-game, at stage 6, is also read on three lanes. Each step is this page’s reading of what the project reports about itself, or of the source its row names, and an empty meter means no figure is reported. Blue marks the steps that meet the playable bar: 20 frames a second and 30 minutes without a crash. A project counted as playable, at stage 7, keeps its lanes and is read against the next goal instead, which blue then marks: 30 frames a second or more and an hour or more without a crash.

Rows at those stages are ordered by their lanes and nothing else, each stage on its own: fewest steps short of the bar, or of the next goal, on speed and stability together, then the better picture, the higher frame rate and the longer session. The lanes were suggested in issue 8 on this page’s repository.

Picture

What the project says is on screen in the game's world.

  1. 1Mostly dark or unlit
  2. 2Lit where the sun falls, shadows black or dark
  3. 3Lit in light and shade, with visible faults
  4. 4Lit and whole, small faults only
  5. 5The console's picture, ray tracing included

Speed

The average it reports over a session of play of 10 minutes or more, or with no such session the middle of the frame rates it reports in play, in rendered frames a second (fps).

  2. 22 fps or more
  3. 35 fps or more
  4. 410 fps or more
  5. 520 fps or more
  6. 630 fps or more, the console's rate

The longest session it reports without a crash.

  1. 1Under 10 minutes

Method and limits

  • Playable builds

    One by its own report, none verified: Vice-Tay documents a 30-minute session at a median of 21.1 frames a second on its own test PC. This page links to projects' source code only and to no build or download. On 26 November VGTimes reported that a packaged build attributed to CGBJ was circulating, which nobody has verified. Downloads offered as GTA VI for PC have carried malware since August.

  • The build

    These projects work from a leaked Xbox build. Press reports of 25 and 26 November say a PlayStation 5 build has leaked too; no listed project reports running it on a PC. Rockstar has not released or announced a PC version. This page does not link to either leak or explain how to get or run it.

  • Sources

    Each project's public repository and open pull requests and, where marked, its chat. Project names link to those repositories to credit the work.

  • Stage claims

    Every stage is the project's own claim unless its source line says otherwise, and most are unverified. CGBJ's comes from press reports, because its code is private.

  • Frame rates

    Rendered frames. Frames added by frame generation are reported as such and do not count toward the playable bar.

  • Lanes

    A project at stage 6 or 7 is also read on three lanes: picture, speed and stability. Each step is this page's reading of what the project reports about itself, and the key under the standings title lists the steps. Speed is the average a project reports over its newest session of play of 10 minutes or more, or its median where it gives no average. A project that reports no such session is read from the middle of the frame rates it reports in play, where a low figure counts if it is given for a stretch or a condition of play and single frames do not. Scripted or automated runs and scenes standing still do not count as play. Where a project logs a session of play minute by minute and gives no figure for the whole session, speed is the mean of the minute medians, which is this page's arithmetic. A project counted as playable, at stage 7, is read against the next goal instead of the playable bar: one session of play of an hour or more at 30 frames a second or better, rendered frames only, which are the top steps of the speed and stability lanes.

  • Tested on

    The systems and graphics card makers a project or its testers report running it on. It may run on others, and a row with none listed has reported none.

  • Crash reports

    By one project's notes, the game's crash reporter sends data to an outside server each time it crashes.

  • Who compiles it

    An AI assistant (Claude), automatically, about every 30 minutes. It can be wrong.

  • Affiliation

    None with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Microsoft or any project listed, nor with any site or tool that reuses these standings.

  • Positions

    Rows at stage 6 and at stage 7 are each ordered by their lanes alone: fewest steps short of the playable bar (stage 6) or of the next goal (stage 7) on speed and stability together, then the better picture, the higher frame rate and the longer session. Lower stages are ordered by this page's reading of what remains before the next stage. A place is computed from what each project reports about itself: it is not a verification or an endorsement. An equals sign means level.

  • Requests

    To ask for a project to be added, corrected or taken off this page, open an issue on its repository.
